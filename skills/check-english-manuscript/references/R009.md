# EN-R009：観察・解釈・因果・主張の強さ

観察・解釈・結論が根拠と対応するかを点検する。
原文の主張の強さ、因果方向、一般化範囲を保ち、根拠の不足は質問に分ける。

## 例

> The detector gain and exposure time both changed. Their effects were not separated. The gain alone caused the higher photon count.

著者への問い：What evidence separates the effect of gain from that of exposure time?
